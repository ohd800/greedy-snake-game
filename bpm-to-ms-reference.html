How BPM converts to milliseconds

There are 60,000 milliseconds in a minute, so one beat lasts 60000 ÷ BPM. At 120 BPM a beat is 500 ms; everything else scales from there. This reference just pre-computes the common tempos so you can grab a number without opening a calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert any BPM to milliseconds?
One beat = 60,000 ÷ BPM ms. At 120 BPM that is 500 ms per quarter note; at 128 BPM it is 468.75 ms. Other note values scale from the beat.
Why list so many tempos?
Musicians search by the exact tempo they are working at — 128 for house, 174 for drum and bass, 90 for hip-hop. Each tempo gets its own page so the table is instantly copyable.
