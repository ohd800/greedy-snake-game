Andante Tempo (BPM)

Andante is an Italian tempo marking meaning walking pace. In modern metronome terms it is generally taken as about 76–108 BPM — a walking-pace tempo, steady and moderate, the everyday pulse of much classical music.

Frequently asked questions

What BPM is Andante?
Andante is generally taken as about 76–108 BPM — walking pace, slower than moderato, faster than adagio.
Is Andante faster or slower than Andantino?
Andante is slower than Andantino. Andantino sits around 80–108 BPM, while Andante is about 76–108 BPM, so Andante is the calmer of the two.
What is one beat in milliseconds at Andante?
At a typical 92 BPM, one beat (a quarter note) is 60000 ÷ 92 ≈ 652.17 ms. At 108 BPM it is about 555.56 ms; at 76 BPM about 789.47 ms.
