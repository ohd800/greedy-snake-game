Grave Tempo (BPM)

Grave is an Italian tempo marking meaning very slow and solemn. In modern metronome terms it is generally taken as about 25–45 BPM — the slowest of the common markings, reserved for the most solemn, funeral-like music.

Frequently asked questions

What BPM is Grave?
Grave is generally taken as about 25–45 BPM — very slow, solemn, the slowest of the common markings.
Is Grave faster or slower than Largo?
Grave is slower than Largo. Largo sits around 40–60 BPM, while Grave is about 25–45 BPM, so Grave is the calmer of the two.
What is one beat in milliseconds at Grave?
At a typical 40 BPM, one beat (a quarter note) is 60000 ÷ 40 ≈ 1500.00 ms. At 45 BPM it is about 1333.33 ms; at 25 BPM about 2400.00 ms.
