Larghetto Tempo (BPM)

Larghetto is an Italian tempo marking meaning rather broad. In modern metronome terms it is generally taken as about 60–66 BPM — a little narrower than largo, still slow but with a touch more forward motion.

Frequently asked questions

What BPM is Larghetto?
Larghetto is generally taken as about 60–66 BPM — rather broad, slower than adagio, faster than largo.
Is Larghetto faster or slower than Adagio?
Larghetto is slower than Adagio. Adagio sits around 66–76 BPM, while Larghetto is about 60–66 BPM, so Larghetto is the calmer of the two.
What is one beat in milliseconds at Larghetto?
At a typical 63 BPM, one beat (a quarter note) is 60000 ÷ 63 ≈ 952.38 ms. At 66 BPM it is about 909.09 ms; at 60 BPM about 1000.00 ms.
