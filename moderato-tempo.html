Moderato Tempo (BPM)

Moderato is an Italian tempo marking meaning at a moderate speed. In metronome terms it is generally taken as about 108–120 BPM — the neutral, natural tempo between andante and allegro.

Frequently asked questions

What BPM is moderato?
Moderato is generally taken as about 108–120 BPM — a moderate, natural speed between andante and allegro.
Is moderato faster or slower than allegro?
Slower. Allegro starts around 120 BPM, while moderato tops out near 120 BPM, so allegro is the quicker of the two.
What is one beat in milliseconds at moderato?
At a typical 114 BPM, one beat (a quarter note) is 60000 ÷ 114 ≈ 526 ms. At 120 BPM it is 500 ms; at 108 BPM about 556 ms.
