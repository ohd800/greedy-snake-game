How RGB to HEX conversion works

HEX is simply base-16 notation for the same three channels. Each channel (0–255) becomes a two-digit hexadecimal pair:

#RRGGBB  where each pair = value.toString(16).padStart(2, '0')

Worked example. Convert rgb(11, 107, 203):

  • Red 11  → 11 ÷ 16 = 0 remainder 11 → 0B
  • Green 107 → 107 ÷ 16 = 6 remainder 11 → 6B
  • Blue 203 → 203 ÷ 16 = 12 remainder 11 → CB

Result: #0B6BCB

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert RGB to HEX by hand?
Convert each of the red, green and blue values (0–255) to a two digit base-16 number, then join them with a leading hash. For example rgb(11, 107, 203) becomes #0B6BCB.
What happens if I enter a value above 255?
Values are clamped to the valid 0–255 range, so 300 is treated as 255. The converter shows a note whenever clamping occurs so you always know the real output.
Does this tool support 3-digit HEX codes?
Yes. Shorthand codes such as #0af are expanded to #00AAFF, and you can enter them with or without the leading hash.
Is my data uploaded anywhere?
No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser; no color value is ever sent to a server.
