Adagio Tempo (BPM)

Adagio is an Italian tempo marking meaning slow and stately. In modern metronome terms it is generally taken as about 66–76 BPM — slower than andante, faster than largo.

Frequently asked questions

What BPM is adagio?
Adagio is generally taken as about 66–76 BPM — slow and stately, slower than andante and faster than largo.
Is adagio faster or slower than andante?
Slower. Andante is roughly 76–108 BPM, while adagio is about 66–76 BPM, so adagio is the calmer of the two.
What is one beat in milliseconds at adagio?
At a typical 70 BPM, one beat (a quarter note) is 60000 ÷ 70 ≈ 857 ms. At 76 BPM it is about 789 ms; at 66 BPM about 909 ms.
