Prestissimo Tempo (BPM)

Prestissimo is an Italian tempo marking meaning as fast as possible. In modern metronome terms it is generally taken as about 200–208 BPM — as fast as possible, the upper limit of what players can cleanly execute.

Frequently asked questions

What BPM is Prestissimo?
Prestissimo is generally taken as about 200–208 BPM — as fast as possible, the fastest marking of all.
Is Prestissimo faster or slower than Presto?
Prestissimo is faster than Presto. Presto sits around 168–200 BPM, while Prestissimo is about 200–208 BPM, so Prestissimo is the quicker of the two.
What is one beat in milliseconds at Prestissimo?
At a typical 204 BPM, one beat (a quarter note) is 60000 ÷ 204 ≈ 294.12 ms. At 208 BPM it is about 288.46 ms; at 200 BPM about 300.00 ms.
