Reporting a conversion error

To fix a bug quickly, it helps to include:

  • Which tool it was (the page URL)
  • The exact value you entered
  • The result you got
  • The result you expected, and why

That is usually enough to reproduce and fix it within a day or two.

Suggesting a new tool

Tool requests are welcome. The most useful suggestions describe the real situation where the conversion was needed — for example "I need to work out delay times in milliseconds from a track's BPM" — rather than just naming a unit pair.
