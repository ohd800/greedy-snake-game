Allegretto Tempo (BPM)

Allegretto is an Italian tempo marking meaning fairly quick. In modern metronome terms it is generally taken as about 100–120 BPM — fairly quick and bright, lighter than allegro and not as driven.

Frequently asked questions

What BPM is Allegretto?
Allegretto is generally taken as about 100–120 BPM — fairly quick, lighter than allegro, close to moderato.
Is Allegretto faster or slower than Allegro?
Allegretto is slower than Allegro. Allegro sits around 120–156 BPM, while Allegretto is about 100–120 BPM, so Allegretto is the calmer of the two.
What is one beat in milliseconds at Allegretto?
At a typical 110 BPM, one beat (a quarter note) is 60000 ÷ 110 ≈ 545.45 ms. At 120 BPM it is about 500.00 ms; at 100 BPM about 600.00 ms.
