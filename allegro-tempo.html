Allegro Tempo (BPM)

Allegro is an Italian tempo marking meaning fast and cheerful. In modern metronome terms it is generally taken as about 120–156 BPM — fast and cheerful, energetic, the workhorse fast tempo of classical music.

Frequently asked questions

What BPM is Allegro?
Allegro is generally taken as about 120–156 BPM — fast, cheerful, faster than allegretto, slower than vivace.
Is Allegro faster or slower than Vivace?
Allegro is slower than Vivace. Vivace sits around 156–176 BPM, while Allegro is about 120–156 BPM, so Allegro is the calmer of the two.
What is one beat in milliseconds at Allegro?
At a typical 138 BPM, one beat (a quarter note) is 60000 ÷ 138 ≈ 434.78 ms. At 156 BPM it is about 384.62 ms; at 120 BPM about 500.00 ms.
