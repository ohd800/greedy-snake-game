Presto Tempo (BPM)

Presto is an Italian tempo marking meaning very fast. In modern metronome terms it is generally taken as about 168–200 BPM — very fast, rushing, the tempo of virtuoso showpieces.

Frequently asked questions

What BPM is Presto?
Presto is generally taken as about 168–200 BPM — very fast, faster than vivace, slower than prestissimo.
Is Presto faster or slower than Prestissimo?
Presto is slower than Prestissimo. Prestissimo sits around 200–208 BPM, while Presto is about 168–200 BPM, so Presto is the calmer of the two.
What is one beat in milliseconds at Presto?
At a typical 184 BPM, one beat (a quarter note) is 60000 ÷ 184 ≈ 326.09 ms. At 200 BPM it is about 300.00 ms; at 168 BPM about 357.14 ms.
