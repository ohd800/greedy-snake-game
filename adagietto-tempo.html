Adagietto Tempo (BPM)

Adagietto is an Italian tempo marking meaning slightly faster than adagio. In modern metronome terms it is generally taken as about 70–80 BPM — a shade quicker and more tender than adagio.

Frequently asked questions

What BPM is Adagietto?
Adagietto is generally taken as about 70–80 BPM — slightly faster than adagio, a shade faster than adagio.
Is Adagietto faster or slower than Andante?
Adagietto is slower than Andante. Andante sits around 76–108 BPM, while Adagietto is about 70–80 BPM, so Adagietto is the calmer of the two.
What is one beat in milliseconds at Adagietto?
At a typical 75 BPM, one beat (a quarter note) is 60000 ÷ 75 ≈ 800.00 ms. At 80 BPM it is about 750.00 ms; at 70 BPM about 857.14 ms.
