Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

The short version

Every conversion on this site runs inside your own browser. The text, colors, timestamps and files you enter are never uploaded to our servers, because there is no server-side processing at all. We do not see your input, and we do not store it.

What we collect

We do not collect the values you type into our tools. Those stay in your browser's memory and disappear when you close the tab.

Like virtually every website, we may collect a small amount of technical information needed to keep the site running and to understand which tools are useful:

  • Usage analytics. Pages visited, approximate country, device type and browser. Used in aggregate only.
  • Server logs. Standard web server logs (IP address, requested URL, timestamp) retained for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

Cookies and advertising

We may display advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies — including the DoubleClick cookie — to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party cookies generally at aboutads.info.
  • Visitors in the EEA and UK are shown a consent prompt before any non-essential cookie is set.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, including access, correction, deletion and objection to processing:

  • EEA / UK (GDPR): you may request access to, or erasure of, any personal data we hold, and you may withdraw cookie consent at any time.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): you may request disclosure of the categories of personal information collected and request its deletion. We do not sell personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details on our contact page.

Children's privacy

Our tools are general-purpose utilities and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

External links

Some pages link to third-party sites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site grows. The "Last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version.

If you have questions about this policy, please reach us via the contact page.
