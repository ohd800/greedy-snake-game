Largo Tempo (BPM)

Largo is an Italian tempo marking meaning broad and stately. In modern metronome terms it is generally taken as about 40–60 BPM — a broad, stately tempo that feels wide and calm.

Frequently asked questions

What BPM is Largo?
Largo is generally taken as about 40–60 BPM — broad, stately, slower than larghetto, faster than grave.
Is Largo faster or slower than Larghetto?
Largo is slower than Larghetto. Larghetto sits around 60–66 BPM, while Largo is about 40–60 BPM, so Largo is the calmer of the two.
What is one beat in milliseconds at Largo?
At a typical 50 BPM, one beat (a quarter note) is 60000 ÷ 50 ≈ 1200.00 ms. At 60 BPM it is about 1000.00 ms; at 40 BPM about 1500.00 ms.
