Andantino Tempo (BPM)

Andantino is an Italian tempo marking meaning little andante. In modern metronome terms it is generally taken as about 80–108 BPM — a little andante, gentle and flowing, slightly quicker than andante.

Frequently asked questions

What BPM is Andantino?
Andantino is generally taken as about 80–108 BPM — little andante, slightly quicker than andante.
Is Andantino faster or slower than Moderato?
Andantino is slower than Moderato. Moderato sits around 108–120 BPM, while Andantino is about 80–108 BPM, so Andantino is the calmer of the two.
What is one beat in milliseconds at Andantino?
At a typical 94 BPM, one beat (a quarter note) is 60000 ÷ 94 ≈ 638.30 ms. At 108 BPM it is about 555.56 ms; at 80 BPM about 750.00 ms.
