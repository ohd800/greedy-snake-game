Vivo / Vivacissimo Tempo (BPM)

Vivo (and Vivacissimo) are Italian tempo markings meaning very lively. In modern metronome terms they are generally taken as about 172–176 BPM — very lively indeed, restless and pushing toward presto.

Frequently asked questions

What BPM is Vivo / Vivacissimo?
Vivo / Vivacissimo is generally taken as about 172–176 BPM — very lively, near presto.
Is Vivo / Vivacissimo faster or slower than Presto?
Vivo / Vivacissimo is slower than Presto. Presto sits around 168–200 BPM, while Vivo / Vivacissimo is about 172–176 BPM, so Vivo / Vivacissimo is the calmer of the two.
What is one beat in milliseconds at Vivo / Vivacissimo?
At a typical 174 BPM, one beat (a quarter note) is 60000 ÷ 174 ≈ 344.83 ms. At 176 BPM it is about 340.91 ms; at 172 BPM about 348.84 ms.
