Vivace Tempo (BPM)

Vivace is an Italian tempo marking meaning lively. In modern metronome terms it is generally taken as about 156–176 BPM — lively and quick, vivid, almost dancelike.

Frequently asked questions

What BPM is Vivace?
Vivace is generally taken as about 156–176 BPM — lively, faster than allegro, close to presto.
Is Vivace faster or slower than Vivo / Vivacissimo?
Vivace is slower than Vivo / Vivacissimo. Vivo / Vivacissimo sits around 172–176 BPM, while Vivace is about 156–176 BPM, so Vivace is the calmer of the two.
What is one beat in milliseconds at Vivace?
At a typical 166 BPM, one beat (a quarter note) is 60000 ÷ 166 ≈ 361.45 ms. At 176 BPM it is about 340.91 ms; at 156 BPM about 384.62 ms.
